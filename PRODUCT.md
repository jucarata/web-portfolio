# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro

## Users

Two audiences, equal weight:

- Hiring managers and recruiters deciding whether to hire Juan Camilo Ramirez.
- Clients and companies deciding whether to commission a software solution from him.

They arrive with little prior context, scan a single page, and leave able to judge both the engineer and the kind of work he takes on.

## Product Purpose

A single-page personal portfolio for Juan Camilo Ramirez (Jucarata). Home acts as About. The page also includes Projects, Experience, Education, and Contact me. Success means a first-time visitor understands who he is, what he builds, and how to reach him, without leaving the page.

## Positioning

Software engineer and software-solutions expert spanning architecture, backend, artificial intelligence, and systems integration. The confirmed line, translated from the Spanish brand manual for this English site:

> Jucarata | Software Engineer & Technology Solutions. I build robust, scalable, and intelligent systems that turn complex requirements into efficient, high-performance software.

Essence: scalability, precision, solidity, and structured innovation. Values: precision, end-to-end versatility, and evolution through technology and AI.

## Operating Context

One scrolling page. Visitors move from About through Projects, Experience, and Education, then to Contact. Experience, education, the summary, skills, location, and email are the supplied record. Projects stay placeholders. No phone number is published. No deploy target is decided.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One page. Sections, in order: Home (About), Projects, Experience, Education, Contact me.
- Site language is English.
- Projects stay labeled placeholder. Do not invent case studies, metrics, or clients. Do not publish a phone number.
- Use the supplied logo and professional banner.
- Minimal. Industry-standard portfolio structure, with the brand manual kept intact.
- Undecided: real case studies, and a YouTube channel if one is supplied later.

## Brand Commitments

- Name / username: Jucarata. Legal name: Juan Camilo Ramirez Tabares. Role: Software Engineer. Based in Cali, Colombia.
- Voice: professional, analytical, confident, oriented to complex engineering problems.
- Official palette: Deep Black `#242021`, Energetic Red `#DE343A`, Innovation Blue `#565FE0`, Creative Yellow `#FFF53B`, White `#FFFFFF`.
- Logo: minimal modular geometric blocks in those four colors, standing for systematic construction and scale. Keep clear space around the icon and the word "JUCARATA". Usable on light grounds and on `#242021`.
- Assets: `assets/logo.jpeg`, `assets/banner.jpeg`.
- Type direction, not a locked face: geometric, clean, modern, highly legible. The manual names Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, and Montserrat as examples. Headers semibold or bold; section labels medium; body regular or light.

## Evidence on Hand

- `brand-manual.md` — identity, palette, type direction, logo rules, voice, and the Spanish value proposition.
- `assets/logo.jpeg` — geometric mark on a light ground.
- `assets/banner.jpeg` — "JUAN CAMILO RAMIREZ" with "Software Engineer" and the mark.
- Absent, and not to be fabricated as real: project case studies, testimonials, and metrics. A phone number is known and must stay off the site.

## Product Principles

- Show the person and the work before any claim.
- Keep both hiring and commissioning paths equally reachable.
- Prefer structure and precision over decoration.
- Label anything synthetic until real material replaces it.
- Leave the brand name, palette, logo, and voice unchanged.
